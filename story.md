# The Saltwick Affair — Story Bible

## Premise

A murder mystery 2D detective game. You play as **Fred Calloway**, a washed-up private investigator called to the coastal town of **Saltwick** after a prominent local figure is found dead. The twist: every person in town you can talk to is powered by a different LLM — each with a distinct personality, agenda, and way of speaking. Some will help you. Some will lie to their teeth. You have to figure out who to believe.

---

## The World: Saltwick

A grey, salt-bitten fishing town on the northern coast. The kind of place people come to disappear. Three streets, a harbour, a lighthouse nobody uses anymore, and one inn that smells like old wood and fish. The locals don't trust outsiders. The fog rolls in by four in the afternoon and doesn't leave until noon the next day.

Saltwick has one claim to fame: the **Vanthorpe Institute**, a private research retreat perched on the cliffs at the edge of town. Think-tanks, philosophers, AI researchers, writers — the kind of people who pay good money to be left alone to think. It's been operating for seven years. The locals tolerate it. Mostly.

---

## The Murder

**Victim: Dr. Maren Sollis, 54**

Founder of the Vanthorpe Institute. Brilliant, polarising, feared. She built the institute from nothing and ran it like a personal fiefdom. Three days ago, she was found dead in her study at the institute — slumped over her desk, a glass of port beside her. The official line from local police: cardiac event. Natural causes.

Her assistant, **Pip**, called you. Said the death was wrong. Said things had been strange at the institute for weeks. Said Maren had been scared of something.

You arrive the morning after the funeral. Everyone has already started moving on. That's your first clue.

---

## The Suspects

Each suspect is an NPC powered by a different LLM. Their in-world personality is distinct. Their information is real but filtered through who they are — their biases, their fears, their agendas. None of them are fully honest. None of them are fully lying.

---

### 1. PIP — Maren's Personal Assistant
*Personality: Eager, anxious, over-precise. Speaks in lists. Corrects himself mid-sentence.*

Pip has been with Maren for four years. He knows her schedule, her habits, her enemies. He's the one who called you, which means he either genuinely wants justice or he's controlling the investigation from the start. He has access to everything — her calendar, her correspondence, her office — and he's very careful about what he volunteers. Talks a lot. Says very little.

**What he knows:** Maren had a meeting the night she died, off the books. He doesn't know with who. He saw a light on in the boathouse at midnight.

**What he's hiding:** He read her emails. He knows about the money.

---

### 2. CALLUM VANTHORPE — Institute Patron & Financier
*Personality: Smooth, patrician, always slightly amused. Never gives a direct answer. Every sentence lands like it was rehearsed.*

Old money. Gave the institute its name and its funding. Technically Maren's boss, though she'd have laughed at that framing. He's in Saltwick for the week to "handle the transition" — already talking to lawyers about succession. Arrived the morning after Maren's death. Very convenient.

**What he knows:** The institute is in financial trouble. Maren had found out something about where the money was going.

**What he's hiding:** He was blackmailing a researcher at the institute, using Maren as cover. When she found out, she threatened to go public.

---

### 3. DR. YUSRA OKAFOR — Resident Researcher, Cognitive Science
*Personality: Blunt, impatient, intellectually arrogant. Has no time for pleasantries. Will tell you the truth but frame it so it sounds like you're stupid for asking.*

Has been at the institute for two years. Didn't like Maren — thought she was more interested in prestige than actual research. But she respected her, in the way you respect an obstacle. She was the last person confirmed to have spoken to Maren the night she died, at dinner.

**What she knows:** Maren seemed distracted at dinner. Checked her phone three times. Left early, said she was tired.

**What she's hiding:** She and Maren had a screaming argument two weeks ago over a research paper Maren tried to suppress. Yusra doesn't think it's relevant. She's wrong.

---

### 4. OSKAR BREL — Town Fisherman & Longtime Local
*Personality: Laconic, suspicious of outsiders, speaks in half-sentences. Has to be asked the same question twice before he'll answer it properly. Deeply observant.*

Born in Saltwick. Has been fishing these waters for thirty years. Doesn't care about the institute but he watches it — from the harbour, from the water. He sees what happens at the cliffs. He saw a car parked near the institute gate the night Maren died, engine running, at 11 PM. Stayed for forty minutes. Left.

**What he knows:** The car's make and a partial plate. He's seen it before.

**What he's hiding:** Nothing, actually. He's one of the few people telling the full truth. The problem is he doesn't realize what he saw matters.

---

### 5. LENA SOLLIS — Maren's Daughter, 28
*Personality: Guarded, grieving, angry in the way people get when they've been angry for years before the grief arrived. Occasionally lets something real slip, then pulls back fast.*

Estranged from her mother for six years. Came back for the funeral. Is supposed to leave tomorrow but hasn't booked her train. She and Maren had reconnected slightly — a few emails, one phone call — in the months before the death. Lena doesn't know what to do with that. She's suspicious of everyone at the institute. She thinks they used her mother.

**What she knows:** Her mother called her three weeks ago, which was unusual. Said she was going to "set things right" with someone. Wouldn't say more.

**What she's hiding:** She came to Saltwick two days before the death — before she was supposed to. She hasn't told anyone. She saw something she doesn't want to be the person who saw it.

---

### 6. CONSTABLE ALDRIC FENN — Local Police
*Personality: Defensive, territorial, by-the-book in a way that tips into laziness. Not corrupt — just not curious. Thinks the case is closed and resents the implication that it isn't.*

The officer who filed the initial report. Declared it natural causes based on the doctor's assessment. Hasn't interviewed anyone beyond the basics. He's not covering anything up — he just doesn't care enough to look deeper, and he doesn't like being shown that he should have.

**What he knows:** The toxicology wasn't run because the doctor said it wasn't needed.

**What he's hiding:** He owes Callum Vanthorpe a favour — Vanthorpe helped fund some equipment for the station two years ago. He doesn't think that affected his judgment. It did.

---

## The Truth

**Callum Vanthorpe poisoned Maren Sollis.**

He'd been siphoning money from the institute for years through shell companies. Maren had uncovered it three weeks before she died and gave him an ultimatum: return the money quietly or she'd expose him. He stalled. She stopped stalling first.

The night she died, she sent him a final message: she was going to her solicitor in the morning. He drove to the institute — that's the car Oskar saw — and met her in her study. He'd brought a drug, something that would look like a cardiac event, slipped into a drink. She trusted him enough to pour him a glass. He swapped them.

The suppressed research paper that Yusra and Maren argued about is a red herring — it matters but not to the murder. The off-books meeting Pip noticed was Maren meeting with a journalist the week before. The journalist hasn't come forward. Yet.

Lena saw Callum's car leaving the institute at midnight. She didn't know it was his at the time. She went for a walk, couldn't sleep. She's been sitting on this because she's not sure what it means, and because she doesn't want to be the one who turns a sad death into a scandal.

## The Case Beats

1. **Arrive in Saltwick.** Talk to Pip. See the institute. Get the lay of the land.
2. **First round of interviews.** Each NPC gives partial information. The picture is murky.
3. **Discover the financial irregularities** — through Maren's documents, or through pressing Callum.
4. **Find out about Oskar's car.** Cross-reference with Callum's alibi (he said he was at his hotel all night).
5. **Uncover Lena's secret visit.** She saw the car leave. She can ID the model. Together with Oskar's partial plate it points to Callum.
6. **Confront Callum.** He's smooth. He'll try to reframe everything. He has alternate explanations for all of it. This is where the player has to have been paying attention.
7. **The break:** Pip, if fully trusted, reveals the email exchange between Maren and Callum — the ultimatum. He'd been sitting on it because he was afraid.
8. **Resolution.** Enough evidence assembled, Constable Fenn has no choice but to make the arrest. He's not happy about it. Neither is Callum.

---

## Tone

Slow burn. Foggy. Quiet. The kind of mystery where the horror is in how normal everyone acts. No jump scares, no gunfights. The gameplay loop is: explore, talk, listen carefully, notice what doesn't add up, come back and ask again. The NPCs remember what you've said. They react to what you've discovered. The world is small but deep.

Think: *Disco Elysium* pacing meets *Agatha Christie* structure, in a Godot 2D world.

---

## NPC Interaction Notes (for implementation later)

Each NPC has:
- A **first conversation** — what they give you unprompted
- A **follow-up layer** — what they reveal if you ask the right things or show them evidence
- A **breaking point** — the moment they stop being careful, triggered by a specific discovery
- A **lie** — at least one thing they say that isn't true, detectable through contradictions elsewhere

The LLM powering each character should be given a system prompt that encodes their personality, what they know, what they're hiding, and how they respond to pressure. The player shouldn't be able to "jailbreak" them — if asked directly "did you do it," they stay in character.
