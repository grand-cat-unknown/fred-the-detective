## Layout

One floor. Two parallel rows of guest rooms with an east-west main hallway between them. To the **west** of the rooms is the **auction hall** (where the auction actually happened earlier — the floor's central public space). The **laundry chute** is at the north-west corner. The **stairs** (the only access to the floor) are at the south-west corner.

- **11xx row (north side, west → east):** 1101 (Vivian) — 1102 (Royal Suite) — 1103 (vacant)
- **12xx row (south side, west → east):** 1201 (Ghostbusters) — 1202 (Ghostbusters) — 1203 (vacant) — 1204 (Julian)

## Geography Notes

- **Stairs** are at the **south-west corner**, and they're the only access to the floor — no elevator.
- The **auction hall** sits west of the rooms, in the middle (vertically between the chute and the stairs). It's the floor's central public space — guests, staff, and the Ghostbusters all pass through it to reach the room hallway.
- The **laundry chute** is at the **north-west corner**, accessible from the auction-hall side and from the service corridor that runs behind the north row.
- A **service corridor** runs along the **north side**, behind the 11xx row. It connects:
  - an access point near **1103** at the east end (which Pemberton can plausibly reach because 1103 is on his sweep checklist),
  - a concealed door into **1102** (the Royal Suite) in the middle,
  - the **laundry chute** and the auction-hall side at the west end.
- Pemberton's actual route on the night:
  - Up the stairs (SW), through the auction hall, into the main hallway.
  - Heads east on his "sweep."
  - Slips into the north service corridor near 1103.
  - Travels west along the corridor to 1102, enters via the concealed door.
  - After the kill, exits back into the corridor and continues west.
  - Drops the cell (with the idol inside) down the chute at the north-west corner.
  - Cuts back south through the auction hall.
  - Emerges into the **west end of the main hallway** at 9:13 — which is why he arrives "from the wrong direction."
- Iris's **west-side observation post** sits at the west end of the main hallway (near the auction-hall entrance), listening to spillover through the wall toward 1102. Her listening device is what catches the chute thud at 9:10.
