# Timeline

Canonical, minute-by-minute timeline for the Sedgewick Haunting case. This is the source of truth — `Story (Clean).md`, `Character sheets.md`, and dialogue/conversation gates should all align to this document.

## How To Read This

- **Actual** column = what really happened (author truth).
- **Public / Witness** column = what each character saw, said, or believed at the time.
- Times are absolute (all "PM" unless noted).
- Open questions are listed at the bottom — anything not pinned down should be answered before code changes start.

## Open Questions

Maybe I just miscounted. I'm sorry. I'm clumsy."* The yesterday-confidence-vs-evening-shortfall is the clue.
2. **The Royal Suite suggestion — resolved.** Vivian does remember, but **casually** — not as an obvious clue. If Fred asks who picked the Royal Suite for Vance, she'll say something like *"Otis suggested it, I think? Or maybe it was my idea. He mentioned it after the auction — said the room suited the occasion. Either way."* The casualness is the point: it's a clue Fred has to notice is meaningful, not one Vivian flags for him.
3. **Vivian's location during 9:00 – 9:14 — resolved.** Front desk on the bottom floor the entire watch window. Leo is there with her. They alibi each other.
4. **Leo's location after 8:50 — resolved.** Bottom floor, helping Vivian at the front desk. He and Vivian are each other's alibi.
5. **Theo at 9:11 — resolved.** He leaves the cart, comes to the door, and helps Mara and Iris try to force it open. He can corroborate "Pemberton wasn't with us between ~9:00 and 9:13."
6. **Julian as a route witness — resolved.** Julian is not a thud or route witness. His useful truth is that he abandoned the fake-idol swap before the watch began.
7. **Mags's smoke break timing.** She is in 1103 smoking the whole window. Open sub-question: does she slip in right before 9:00 or earlier? Affects only whether she could see Pemberton heading east before her door shuts. **Lean: she's already in by 8:55, sees nothing.**
8. **The 9:11 thud — resolved.** The thud is captured by Iris's abandoned device, not by Iris, Julian, Mags, or a bellhop witness.
9. **Pemberton's radio chatter.** *Skip for now.* Will revisit if the radio log becomes a useful clue surface; otherwise leave it as background texture.
10. **Service-hall discarded gloves — resolved.** The stained gloves are a normal pickup if Fred finds the service corridor. They are supporting evidence, not a progression gate.
11. **Julian's alibi.** He should not claim "bar with a martini" (puts him off-floor, lie chosen too cleanly). To pick what he *does* claim — possibly "I was in my room, I heard the commotion and locked my door," which is mostly true and only weird because of what's *in* his room. **TBD.**

## Continuity Checks (vs. existing docs)

These are inconsistencies between this draft and the older docs. Flagging for resolution.

- **Mara assignment timing.** This timeline locks assignments at **9:00**, when the watch begins.
- **Iris and "Thorne."** Older docs use both "Iris" and "Thorne" interchangeably for the same character. Pick one ("Iris" preferred — first names match the rest of the cast).
- **Chute event source.** Iris does not personally witness the 9:11 chute-side event. She leaves her listening device at the west post and runs east to converge on the 1102 front door before it happens. The device, left behind on the post, logs the event. Iris's own account should stay narrow: she was at the west post with her equipment, then she went to the door.
- **Glove swap timing.** The stained gloves are discarded in the service hall **before** the cell goes down the chute, not after. The chute hatch and the cell in the basement bin only ever bear *fresh-glove* prints. The discarded stained pair is the forensic anchor in the service hall; the chute itself is forensically clean.
- **Pemberton's arrival explanation.** Older docs (`Story (Clean).md`, `Character sheets.md`) have Pemberton say "It crossed through the service route — I cut west to flank it." This timeline removes that line. Pemberton **never** volunteers the service route — doing so would hand the player the hidden-passage idea for free. He arrives from the west with no explanation and mutters something vague if pressed.
- **Mara doesn't know the service routes exist.** Older docs implicitly allow Mara to accept a "flanking through the service hallways" explanation. This timeline locks: **Mara does not know about the service routes.** She registers Otis's wrong-direction arrival as unexplained-and-weird, has no theory to paper it over with, and reports it as such to Fred. (Vivian is the route gatekeeper — see Open Question #10.)
- **Iris's wrong-direction note.** Older docs have Iris explicitly framing the arrival as "despite being assigned east." This timeline softens it: Iris reports only **what she saw** — "Otis arrived behind me / from my side." She does **not** voluntarily layer the "but he was assigned east" contradiction on top. Fred has to combine her account with Mara's assignment record to surface the contradiction himself.
- **Bellhop corroboration removed.** There is no separate Cyril witness. Leo Rossi remains the bellhop in the game and is at the front desk with Vivian during the watch window.
- **Glove reveal mechanic.** Older docs have Pemberton's stains become naturally visible as gloves come off after the crisis. This timeline locks: **the entire team stays gloved through the aftermath** (post-incident contamination protocol). Pemberton's stains are only exposed when **Fred asks the team to show their palms** later in the investigation — a player-driven reveal, not a passive one.
- **Julian's toolkit.** Older docs have Julian carrying a chloroform vial in addition to the fake idol. This timeline **removes the chloroform.** Julian carries only the master key + the fake wax idol. The only damning item in his room is the fake idol.
- **Auction timing.** Older docs imply weeks between the auction and the murder. This timeline locks: **the auction happened last night**, in this hotel's auction hall. Everyone on the floor still has it fresh in mind, which is why Vance's checkout-tomorrow announcement creates immediate pressure for both Pemberton and Julian.
- **The lead-lined pressure chamber.** Older docs lean on the spent Siren Cell's lead-lined chamber masking the idol's PKE signature. This timeline drops that mechanic — the idol is inside the cell only because that's where Pemberton hid it for disposal. The team never PKE-sweeps the basement laundry bin (they think the entity absorbed the idol), so the masking is unnecessary.
- **Pemberton's claimed checkpoint.** He claims to have checked Room 1103 on his east sweep (and 1203 on the south side as a second checkpoint). This timeline has him bypassing both entirely. The 1103 claim is the lie that the lit cigarette inside contradicts.
