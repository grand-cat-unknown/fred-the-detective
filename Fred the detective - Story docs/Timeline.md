# Timeline

Canonical, minute-by-minute timeline for the Sedgewick Haunting case. This is the source of truth — `Story (Clean).md`, `Character sheets.md`, and dialogue/conversation gates should all align to this document.

## How To Read This

- **Actual** column = what really happened (author truth).
- **Public / Witness** column = what each character saw, said, or believed at the time.
- Times are absolute (all "PM" unless noted).
- Open questions are listed at the bottom — anything not pinned down should be answered before code changes start.

## Open Questions

Things this timeline doesn't yet pin down. Resolve before code changes.

1. **Cell theft timing.** Roughly when during gear prep does Pemberton palm the Siren Cell? Is it part of a witnessed-but-unremarkable moment Theo can later remember? (Affects Theo's "Pemberton hovered near the cart" line.)
2. **The Royal Suite suggestion.** Does Vivian remember that Pemberton suggested 1221 for Vance? If yes, that's a discoverable clue — Fred can ask her, and the answer reframes the whole choice of room. If she's forgotten or thinks it was her own idea, the clue is dormant. Pick a posture.
3. **Vivian's location during 9:00 – 9:14.** "Front desk" is a placeholder. Could she be doing rounds, in the office, in the bar? Affects whether she has any incidental observation to contribute.
4. **Leo's location after 8:50.** He needs to be plausibly off the 12th floor for the murder window so he isn't a witness. Concrete service route?
5. **Theo at 9:11.** Does he leave the gear cart to come to the door, or stay with the cart? Affects whether Pemberton could double back to the cart later, and whether Theo can be a corroborating witness for "Pemberton wasn't with us at 9:11."
6. **Julian as an east-side witness.** Julian's suite (1225) is on the east side, the same side as Pemberton's actual route into 1221. Does Julian hear Pemberton's footsteps in the east-side service corridor through his shared wall before 9:06? If yes, he's a potential — but reluctant — route witness, because admitting he heard anything places him "listening at the wall" instead of "in the bar with a martini."
7. **Mags's smoke break timing.** Is she already in the Housekeeping Room when the team arrives, or does she slip in after they set up? Affects whether she could see Pemberton heading east at 9:00.
8. **The 9:10 thud — is it audible to anyone besides Iris?** Mags is close to the chute too. If she hears it, she's a second confirmation; if she doesn't, Iris's device becomes load-bearing.
9. **Pemberton's radio chatter.** Locked toward silence — he gives one early "east sweep, nothing yet" at ~9:05 to plant the cover, then goes quiet. If the team calls him after 9:07 he doesn't answer (he's mid-kill, then mid-disposal). Open sub-question: does the team radio log preserve the missing replies as a discoverable clue, or is it lost?
10. **Gloves disposal.** Service-hall trash can — which one? Reachable from Fred's exploration without solving the case first?

## Continuity Checks (vs. existing docs)

These are inconsistencies between this draft and the older docs. Flagging for resolution.

- **Mara assignment timing.** `Story (Clean).md` §2 says assignments happen at 9:00. `Mara Bell — What She Said` says "I sent Thorne west and Pemberton east **when the readings jumped**." The clue-gating doc already flags this. This timeline locks it at **9:00**.
- **Iris and "Thorne."** Older docs use both "Iris" and "Thorne" interchangeably for the same character. Pick one ("Iris" preferred — first names match the rest of the cast).
- **Who hears the thud.** `Story (Clean).md` §2 line 27 says "Iris hears the thud from the west-side observation post." This timeline overrides that: **Iris does not hear the thud herself.** Her listening device, left behind on the west-side post, captures it. She doesn't know the thud exists until Fred recovers the device and brings it back to her — at which point she decodes the log and reports it. Her own memory of 9:10 is "the noises stopped," nothing more.
- **Pemberton's arrival explanation.** Older docs (`Story (Clean).md`, `Character sheets.md`) have Pemberton say "It crossed through the service route — I cut west to flank it." This timeline removes that line. Pemberton **never** volunteers the service route — doing so would hand the player the hidden-passage idea for free. He arrives from the west with no explanation, mutters something vague if pressed in the moment, and lets the team's adrenaline paper over the direction discrepancy. The wrong-direction arrival is a clue Fred discovers on his own, by comparing Iris's and Mara's accounts.
- **The lead-lined pressure chamber.** Older docs lean on the spent Siren Cell's lead-lined chamber masking the idol's PKE signature. This timeline drops that mechanic — the idol is inside the cell only because that's where Pemberton hid it for disposal. The team never PKE-sweeps the basement laundry bin (they think the entity absorbed the idol), so the masking is unnecessary.
- **Pemberton's claimed checkpoint.** He claims to have checked the Housekeeping Room on his east sweep. This timeline has him bypassing it entirely. That's correct — the claim is the lie.
